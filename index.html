Across enterprise knowledge, localization and AI annotation projects, I've spent years reviewing records at scale — applying labeling guidelines, spotting inconsistencies, cleaning up attributes, and escalating ambiguous cases so classification systems stay accurate as they grow.

For a Taxonomy Analyst role in Labeling Operations, that translates directly into title standardization, occupation mapping, attribute attachment, and refinement of classification outputs for job- and resume-related data.
